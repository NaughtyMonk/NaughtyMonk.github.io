About Me

Hi there! I'm Bogdan Frolov, a 3D Generalist with over 5+ years of experience in bringing ideas to life through detailed 3D models, textures, and animations for VR, games, and cinema.
My expertise spans the entire 3D pipeline, from high-poly modeling and optimization to rigging, skinning, and motion capture. I actively leverage Artificial Intelligence in my work, including AI-driven model, texture, and concept generation with tools like ComfyUI, significantly accelerating and optimizing production processes.
In AAA-projects , I've not only developed assets but also led character teams, allowing me to effectively combine technical proficiency with strong project management, organizational, and communication skills.
I'm always seeking new challenges and growth opportunities, striving to create truly immersive and captivating content. I'm fluent in Czech, English, Russian and Ukrainian.
My skills are backed by certifications, which you can find, along with my full resume detailing my work experience and achievements, further down this page.

My Software Toolkit:

  • 3D Modeling & Sculpting: Blender, 3ds Max, Zbrush, Marvelous Designer, SpeedTree
  • UV & Texturing: Substance Painter, RizomUV
  • Rendering & Baking: Marmoset Toolbag
  • Game Engines: Unreal Engine 4-5, Unity
  • Animation & Motion Capture: Rokoko Studio, Maya
  • Version Control: Git, Azure
  • AI Tools: ComfyUI, ChatGPT, Midjourney, AI-3D generation tools
  • Web-based 3D: A-frame
  • Programming: HTML 5, CSS, Java Script, Python
