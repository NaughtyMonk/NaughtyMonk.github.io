The Curse of the Seas: My Key Contribution to a Pirate Adventure

Project Overview: A Dynamic Naval Adventure

The Curse of the Seas is a PC game about pirate adventures, where the goal is to lift Davy Jones' deadly curse. Players command a ship on a mini-map, engaging in exciting top-down naval battles with unique mechanics: strategic underwater diving for evasion, the ability to flee from battle against superior forces, and powerful cursed cannonballs. The ship evolves with each level, visually transforming and gaining health. This is a fun, relaxing game designed for lighthearted and engaging entertainment.

3D Modeling: Architect of the Visual World

I was responsible for the complete 3D modeling of all key in-game assets. This included crafting all ships and cannons, along with the epic Kraken – a central element of the open seas – and all interactive environmental objects (barrels, crates). All models were executed in a low-polygon style for high optimization and future adaptation for mobile platforms. My approach ensured the efficient creation of all visual content, demonstrating a deep understanding of pipeline best practices and cross-platform requirements.

Level Design: Atmosphere and Innovation

My role in level design involved creating an authentic Caribbean island atmosphere and functionally integrating gameplay mechanics. The inclusion of the Kraken, animated using vertex animation, became a pivotal element that is not only visually impressive but also showcases my innovative approach to optimization and design of the game world.

VFX: High-Performance Visual Effects

I designed and implemented all visual effects in the game, including dynamic explosions and ship destruction. The application of vertex animation for VFX was my unique solution, ensuring exceptional optimization without sacrificing visual quality. This proves my deep understanding of the game engine (Unreal Engine) and ability to find highly effective technical solutions.

Voice Acting: Crafting the Game's Atmosphere

I performed the voice acting for the pirate crew, lending a humorous and lighthearted tone to their lines. My work on the audio helped reinforce the game's comedic and relaxing atmosphere, demonstrating my contribution to both the visual and auditory player experience.

Multifunctional Specialist in a Lean Team

Within a small team comprising programmers, a UI/UX specialist, and myself, I took on a comprehensive role spanning 3D art, level design, and VFX. My ability to effectively work across multiple disciplines, make strategic optimization decisions (as with low-poly models and vertex animation), and actively shape the overall game experience, underscores my status as a valuable and multifunctional specialist. This project serves as a testament to creating dynamic and engaging products with high-quality execution, even with limited resources.

Magic Madness (VR): My Core Contribution to a VR PvP Arena

Project Overview: Dynamic VR PvP Battles

Magic Madness is an online multiplayer VR PvP game where up to 8 players clash in magical arenas. The core mechanic involves hand-drawing magical signs to cast spells. The project offers intense fantasy battles with full immersion.

Leadership and Innovation in the Character Department

As the Character Department Team Lead (a team of 6), I was responsible for all technical aspects and art quality. I actively implemented know-how: rapid retopology, universal materials in Unreal Engine, and optimization of the character creation pipeline. My role was critical in establishing effective work standards.

3D Modeling and VR Optimization

My department developed 100% of the game's 40 unique characters. I personally created 25% of these characters from concept to implementation. For VR performance, I established and verified strict metrics: up to 20,000 polygons, 3 materials, and 70 bones per character. Using AI for concepts, ZBrush, Blender, Substance Painter, I ensured high-quality yet maximally optimized models with custom LODs.

Rigging and Animation: Unification and Performance

I led rigging and animation, implementing a unified rig for all characters compatible with Unreal Engine VR. This optimized animation production and established a single standard. My contribution included personally recording 30% of animations via mocap and setting up their playback, including the spell-drawing system.

Unreal Engine 5 Integration: Technical Excellence

I was responsible for detailed character setup in UE5: animations, materials, customization, and collision. My integration approach focused on maximum performance, avoiding unoptimized UE5 features unsuitable for VR. The unified character standard greatly simplified programmers' work on core spell-drawing mechanics.

Documentation Creation: Foundation for an Effective Team

I developed key documentation for all related departments: concept guidelines and detailed technical character requirements. This increased pipeline transparency, improved team collaboration, and ensured assets met high quality and optimization standards.

Challenges of Multiverse and Customization

One of the most complex challenges was creating a multiverse with diverse character styles while maintaining a unified identity. The main technical achievement was the realization of a real-time VR character customizer – a task demanding massive optimization and meticulous development. My experience demonstrates the ability to bring projects to technical perfection under ambitious requirements.

CQB Arena VR: My Contribution to a Realistic Training Simulator for Meta Quest

Project Overview: High-Fidelity VR Training

CQB Arena VR is a unique VR simulator for the Meta Quest Store, designed to train military personnel in clearing buildings of terrorists. The game offers dynamic missions focused on quickly eliminating enemies and rescuing hostages against the clock, with penalty time for errors. Training locations recreate an office environment and a parking lot, utilizing modular elements and stylized "cardboard" targets for enemies and hostages. The project features super-realistic weapon interaction, where reloading and manipulation are performed with the player's hands, ensuring full immersion.

3D Modeling of Environment and Props: Creating a Functional Space

I was responsible for creating various props to fill the environment, used to build functional training ranges. My work included creating diverse office furniture (desks, chairs, reception elements, kitchen equipment). The goal was to create functional assets that provided the necessary immersion for military training. I also handled weapon animation and rigging.

VR Optimization and High Detail: Balancing Performance

Given Meta Quest's requirements, optimization was a critical task. I strictly controlled technical parameters, ensuring no more than 300-350 draw calls per viewport and a polygon count of up to 700,000 triangles per viewport. A special budget was allocated for super-realistic weapons, which are constantly in the player's view. Trim sheets were used for texture optimization, and custom lightmaps were applied to merged objects to improve shadow quality. My approach allowed for high detail while maintaining optimal performance in VR.

Character Work and Unique Interactions

My contribution to the main character (first-person view, character not visible) involved rigging the player's hands and setting up basic animations, which ensured realistic weapon interaction through hand movements. This task was one of the most challenging, requiring meticulous work to precisely fit weapon models to hand animations for reloading and gripping.

Cardboard Target Stylization and Destruction Physics

I worked with the models of cardboard enemies and hostages, created based on concepts. Bandits appear brutal and armed, while hostages are frightened and unarmed. Enemies react physically when hit, swaying, and upon destruction, realistically shatter into pieces and disappear. Hostages are rescued by aiming and pressing the X button. This combination of visual stylization and interactive physics added a unique element to the gameplay.

Development Pipeline and Cross-Disciplinary Collaboration

My pipeline included thorough reference gathering, blockout, high-poly modeling (ZBrush), retopology, UV unwrapping, baking (Marmoset), texturing (Substance Painter), creating custom collisions, and importing into Unreal Engine 4. I actively collaborated with programmers, ensuring correct pivot setup for interactive elements (e.g., doors) and proper material configurations. I also periodically tested levels for bugs and mechanics, which was critical for seamless 3D asset integration into gameplay.

Realistic Weapons and Attachment Preparation

Although I was not directly involved in weapon modeling, my work included setting up their rigging and animations, as well as preparing attachment assets (flashlights, laser sights, foregrips) with correct sockets for easy integration. Weapons featured realistic recoil and even a "feel" of weight, significantly enhancing player immersion.

Successful Release within Tight Deadlines

This project was developed by our small team (5-6 people) within very tight deadlines, and we are proud that CQB Arena VR was successfully published for free on the Meta Quest Store. The final result – a combination of high-quality graphics, realistic interactions, and engaging gameplay – is a bright testament to effective teamwork and the ability to create successful VR products. My contribution to the creation, optimization, and setup of all visual content was key to this collective success.

Samsara Gnome (PC): Magic of Transformations and Complex Level Design

Project Overview: A Platformer with Unique Transformation Mechanics

Samsara Gnome is an engaging PC mini-game platformer with a side-scrolling view, featuring fully 3D objects rendered in a stylized graphic style. The main goal is to complete a large and complex level, controlling a magical Gnome capable of transforming into creatures he touches. Each creature possesses unique abilities: Gnome (jump, punch), Toad (long jump, vulnerable to coins), Bat (straight flight, shoots coins), Chicken (short-range teleportation). The level is filled with traps (spikes, destructible boxes, water) requiring thoughtful transformations and precise trajectory calculations.

Level Design: Thought-Out Challenges and Multi-Locations

I was responsible for the entire level design of the project, creating one large and complex level that demands constant strategic thinking and transformations. The level was designed so that each section required the specific abilities of a certain creature, forcing the player to find and utilize the correct form (e.g., using the Bat to reach the Toad, then jumping over a chasm). Through trial and error, as well as continuous testing (including personal and team efforts), we achieved challenging yet solvable gameplay.

3D Modeling: A Stylized World Inspired by Platforming Classics

I created and integrated all 3D models for the level and characters. Inspired by the dynamic platforming of Crash Bandicoot 1, I aimed to create a similar level of complexity and spatial structure for the levels. The overall visual style was stylized. I personally developed half of all environment assets from scratch, while optimizing and retexturing the other half from marketplaces to match our unique style. The level was entirely built from scratch. The pipeline included concepts, high-poly modeling (when necessary), retopology, UV unwrapping, baking (Marmoset), texturing (Substance Painter). Characters (Gnome, Toad, Bat, Chicken) were created using AI for concept generation, which were then manually refined and re-worked following a standard pipeline (rigging, skinning, animation, collisions, import into Unreal). My approach ensured a cohesive and unique visual style.

Dynamic Environment Switching: Visual Immersion

One of the most interesting mechanics was the dynamic change in level textures and mood during transformations into different creatures. I implemented this through a simple and elegant level swapping mechanism upon creature switching, creating 4 unique visual styles: dark (Bat), underwater (Toad), normal (Gnome), and bright/purple/humorous (Chicken). This required meticulous material setup and attention to detail to avoid collision issues during transitions.

Audio Design: Crafting Atmosphere Through Sound and Music

I was responsible for the entire audio style of the project, selecting and adjusting sounds from libraries. I personally generated voices and random phrases for each creature using AI, striving to match their character as closely as possible. I implemented dynamic music changes during transformations, ensuring smooth transitions between tracks. I set up the spatial audio for objects (e.g., coin pick-up sound) via Blueprints in Unreal Engine, linking audio components with collisions for precise playback.

VFX: Visual Effects for Gameplay and Atmosphere

I created various VFX, including those that enhanced gameplay. For example, the fire effect on coins when playing as the Toad visually indicated to the player that these coins should not be collected. I also personally developed and set up the "underwater vision" effect for the Toad, and animated rotating coins. These effects, along with the use of ready-made and adapted ones, significantly improved visual perception and mechanic clarity.

Team Collaboration and Overcoming Challenges

Working with two programmers and a game designer from the very beginning of the project, we collaborated closely using Git for shared work. The biggest challenge was level design, which required a deep understanding of transformation mechanics and constant testing to ensure challenge and navigability. I actively participated in testing, offering ideas and helping identify bugs, which contributed to continuous level improvement.

Key Achievements: Comprehensive Contribution and Unique Mechanics

I am proud to have proven my ability to execute a full cycle of tasks, from 3D modeling (including characters and environments) and animation to audio and VFX design, as well as comprehensive project setup in Unreal Engine. The unique transformation mechanics with dynamic changes to locations, voices, and music make Samsara Gnome a truly memorable project. My experience in a small team leadership role and my comprehensive contribution were key to creating this engaging and technically complex game.
